# Porch lamp, camera lens and viewing cone

Asset: `porchlight-logomark-concept-v3.png`.

New concept on the owner's direction: a wall-mounted porch lamp integrates a camera lens in place of the bulb. A wide curved beam doubles as the camera's field of view. Warm orange/amber dominates, with forest green used at the lens center. Palette direction remains `#EAB951` and `#283F35`.

Created using the built-in image-generation tool and approved by the owner on 2026-10-07. Applied to the loading screen, owner setup/sign-in, desktop sidebar and mobile header. Standard192/512px icons and a padded512px maskable variant are exported using the existing FFmpeg installation; the approved artwork is retained. Browser-tab, home-screen and notification references use versioned paths. A crisp vector master remains future refinement; the generated raster contains tonal variation and edge artifacts.

Republish/export with `.venv/Scripts/python.exe scripts/generate-app-icons.py`, then `npm.cmd --prefix apps/web run build`. No new graphics dependency is needed.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset: a new original Porchlight app logomark, one isolated emblem on a genuinely transparent background.
Concept: a PORCH LIGHT that is also a CAMERA. Design a compact contemporary wall-mounted porch sconce with a short elegantly bent mounting arm and a broad simple rounded lamp hood. Under the hood, integrate a prominent circular CAMERA LENS in place of the bulb: clearly defined thick outer ring, dark center and one restrained highlight. From that lens, a wide tapered beam fans downward and slightly forward, forming the camera's field of view. The beam should be a bold elegant fan-shaped sector with a gently curved outer edge, visually reading both as warm light and a viewing cone. The lamp/lens/beam must form one coherent distinctive silhouette, with clever negative space and substantial geometry.
Brand palette: orange/amber #EAB951 is the main color, accounting for most of the mark. Forest green #283F35 is a small lens-center accent only. Use transparent cutouts to define the lens and separate the viewing beam from the hardware. The mark needs to pop on the app's deep forest-green background.
Style: exceptional professional identity design, friendly yet confident, flat geometric vector-like forms, clean smooth edges, two solid colors, balanced square composition and generous margins. The fixture must read as an outdoor porch lamp rather than a desk lamp or a street light. Simplify to the minimum recognizable forms; readable at app icon size. Beam conveys camera coverage without labels.
Avoid: letters, wordmark, text, house/roof outline, video-camera side tail, P monogram, eye, shield, lock, sparkle, bulb filament, multiple rays, tiny screws, ornamental lantern cage, realistic illustration, gradients, grain, texture, glow, shadows, bevels, 3D, mockups or presentation boards.
Deliver one polished creative logomark only.
