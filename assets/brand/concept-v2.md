# Orange-led home-camera concept

Asset: `porchlight-logomark-concept-v2.png`.

Revised on the owner's feedback: warm orange/amber dominates; an immediately recognizable video camera and home roof communicate home monitoring. A small lens spark suggests smart analysis. The existing `#EAB951` amber and `#283F35` forest green remain the palette direction.

The owner rejected the earlier P/doorway concept. This new concept is a transparent raster proposal generated with the built-in image-generation tool. It has not replaced application assets. The raster includes tonal variation and edge artifacts; a selected direction needs a crisp vector master, small-size checks and dark/light variants before production use.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Create a new original logomark for Porchlight, a home security-camera app that saves video clips locally and uses local AI to explain activity. It must immediately communicate home video monitoring.
Design: one compact unified symbol, a bold rounded VIDEO CAMERA silhouette whose upper body subtly forms a pitched house roof. An unmistakable large circular camera lens occupies its center. Integrate one small four-point spark near the upper-right edge of the lens to suggest intelligent analysis. The roof, camera body, lens and spark should feel like one thoughtfully designed emblem, not a collage of icons. No letter P, no doorway, no hanging lamp.
Color: warm orange/amber is overwhelmingly dominant, drawn from the existing app's #EAB951 accent; forest green #283F35 may be a restrained secondary lens detail. Transparent negative space should provide most interior definition. Keep the logo high contrast against a dark forest-green app interface.
Style: professionally designed contemporary brand mark; confident substantial geometric shapes with friendly rounded corners. Completely flat solid fills, clean smooth vector-like edges, no gradients, bevels, textures, shadows or glow. Simple enough to recognize at 32 pixels. Balanced square composition with generous clear margins.
Output: one isolated logo, genuinely transparent background, no text, no wordmark, no mockup, no presentation board, no multiple options. No eye, shield, padlock, alarm, Wi-Fi waves, tiny details, or generic AI brain.
